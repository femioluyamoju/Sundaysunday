Managing Couples' Resentment (Part Three)

1. Separate Behaviour from Identity: Your partner made a mistake, but that mistake does not define their entire character. Address the problem without attacking the person[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span)[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

2. Eliminate Permanent Verdicts: Avoid words like "always", "never", and "you will never change", which shut down constructive dialogue[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span)[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).

3. Listen Without Defending: True communication happens when both partners feel safe to express hurt without immediately preparing counter-attacks[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
